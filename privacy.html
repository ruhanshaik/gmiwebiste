Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

GMI Tech respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details (name, phone number, email) when you reach out to us.
  • Project details and business information shared during consultations.
  • Website usage data through analytics tools (page visits, browser type, location).
  • WhatsApp message content when you contact us via WhatsApp.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To communicate with you about your project or inquiry.
  • To deliver and improve our services.
  • To send project updates, invoices, and support communications.
  • To improve our website and user experience.
  • We do NOT sell your data to any third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. We use industry-standard encryption and access controls to protect your information. Only authorised GMI Tech team members can access client data, and only for the purpose of delivering services.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools such as WhatsApp, Google Analytics, and Zoho Mail to communicate and operate our business. Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings. No advertising or tracking cookies are used beyond standard analytics.

6. Your Rights

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to request deletion of your data.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to deliver our services and comply with legal obligations. Project data is typically retained for 3 years after project completion.

8. Contact Us

For privacy-related requests, contact us at gmitech@zohomail.in or call +91 90367 17885. We will respond within 48 hours.
